# Retainer inventory capture

Retainer bags and crystals are captured independently. Opening a named retainer's inventory establishes a session only when the selected retainer and the inventory owner agree. Repeated observations must settle for one second; closing the inventory, switching retainer or losing readiness resets pending reads. Quickly opening the retainer list no longer prevents collection: the selected native retainer's name/ID can be recorded without treating an incomplete roster as authoritative or removing other retainers.

A settled native occupied-slot count of zero records known-empty bags, including when unused bag containers are unavailable. Contradictory nonempty reads or unreadable slots block that empty inference. For populated bags, every occupied slot reported by the selected retainer must be accounted for in loaded, readable containers. An unloaded page cannot hide extra stacks once that authoritative count matches; missing stacks keep the previous snapshot. This allows mostly empty inventories to save without waiting for unused pages. Native capacity/counts remain bounded by the 175-slot retainer inventory, independently of the visible window's page arrangement.

Crystals require the complete eighteen-slot crystal container. Unloaded crystals do not prevent bag capture and unloaded bags do not prevent crystal capture. Refreshing one part preserves remembered quantities from the other. New snapshots store part coverage, so an unobserved part remains Missing only for relevant items; observed-empty bags are hidden as known absent. Item and crafting tooltips use the same coverage rules. Older complete snapshots retain their existing quantities and remain readable without a schema-version change.

Settings show bag/crystal snapshot counts and the current capture state, including identity mismatch, incomplete data and settling. Tracking still runs with tooltip features disabled. Data remains local and isolated by character. This update neither clears nor rewrites installed snapshots during release.

Regression checks exercise empty/sparse inventories, last-item removal, contradictory/transitional reads, unavailable crystals, partial bags, repeated unchanged reads, matching/switching identities, window closure, new-retainer discovery, tooltip and recipe results, serialization, old complete snapshots and invalid coverage. Native in-game acceptance remains separate.
